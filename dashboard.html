Healthcare Guide

For educational purposes only. Not a replacement for veterinary advice.

🛁

Dog Shampoo

Use pH-balanced, sulfate-free dog shampoos. Avoid human shampoos.

Recommended: Vet's Best Natural, Burt's Bees Dog Shampoo. Bathe every 4–6 weeks. Use lukewarm water and rinse thoroughly to prevent skin irritation.

🐱

Cat Shampoo

Cats rarely need baths. Use gentle, waterless shampoo if needed.

Most cats self-groom. If bathing is necessary, use fragrance-free cat-specific shampoo. Avoid getting water in ears. Dry thoroughly after bath.

🐾

Paw Moisturizer

Keep paws soft and crack-free with pet-safe balms.

Apply paw balm after walks, especially in hot or cold weather. Check for cracked pads, cuts, or foreign objects. Products like Musher's Secret work well.

✂️

Nail Cutting

Trim nails every 3–4 weeks to prevent overgrowth pain.

Use sharp pet nail clippers. Avoid cutting the quick (pink area). If unsure, ask your vet for a demo. Reward with treats to make it a positive experience.

💇

Hair Trimming

Regular grooming prevents matting and keeps coats healthy.

Brush daily for long-haired breeds. Professional grooming every 6–8 weeks recommended. Use slicker brushes for removing tangles gently.

🦷

Dental Care

Brush teeth 2–3 times weekly to prevent dental disease.

Use pet-specific toothpaste (never human toothpaste). Dental chews and water additives also help. Annual dental check-ups with your vet are important.
