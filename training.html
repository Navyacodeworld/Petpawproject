Featured Tutorial

How to teach "Stay" in 5 minutes

Positive reinforcement is key. Use high-value treats and keep sessions under 10 minutes to avoid frustration.

Command Library

Sit

Hold a treat near their nose, move your hand up. As their head goes up, their bottom goes down.

Stay

Ask for a sit, open the palm of your hand towards them, and say "Stay". Step back slowly.

Down

From a sit, hold a treat and lower it to the floor. Slide it along the ground away from them.
