General Feeding Guidelines

Puppies & Kittens

Needs high protein and frequent meals (3-4 times a day) for growth.

Adult Pets

Typically 2 meals a day. Monitor weight and adjust portions accordingly.

Senior Pets

Slower metabolism. Requires lower-calorie diets and joint supplements.
